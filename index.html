    Why the stair is arranged this way
    • WT §69: max 17 risers in one flight, riser ≤ 190 → floor-to-floor 3.215 m (17 × 189 mm, goings 250)
    • A straight flight needs a bottom landing and a landing in front of the top step: 1.0 + 4.0 + 1.1 m – more than the 4.25 m well
    • So the flight rises south from the entrance hall (secret insulated door, landing inside the well) and lands on the first floor over the living – the last three risers pass over the spine wall inside the media-wall bulkhead
    • The ground-floor bathroom moved next to the utility; its hidden door is in the living-room panelling as agreed
